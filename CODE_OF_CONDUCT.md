# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in Product Page UX Audit to be welcoming, constructive, and respectful.

Examples of expected behavior:

- Be respectful when discussing different design approaches.
- Give specific, actionable criticism rather than personal attacks.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and reasoning.
- Accept constructive feedback and focus on improving the project.
- Respect other contributors' time and perspectives.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, discrimination, intimidation, and deliberately disruptive conduct.

## Maintainers

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may edit, remove, or reject contributions that do not meet them.

Concerns can be raised privately through the contact method listed in the repository owner's GitHub profile.
