# Example: Graphic Design

## Brief

Make a social post announcing a new community garden opening. Required details: “Opening Day,” Saturday, June 14, 10 a.m.–2 p.m., 42 Pine Street, and “Bring a friend.”

## Design direction

Use a photograph of the garden as the background, with a subtle dark overlay behind the text. Set “Opening Day” as the headline, the date and time directly below it, and address and invitation in a smaller grouped footer. Keep one bright green accent for the date marker and callout. Align text to the same left edge and allow open space around the headline.

## Critique example

- **What I see:** The decorative leaf illustrations repeat around the border and compete with the headline.
- **Why it matters:** The event name loses its role as the main focal point at feed size.
- **Suggested change:** Keep one leaf motif near the footer and increase the headline's contrast against the photo.
- **Check:** Confirm all required details remain legible in a small square preview and alternate crop.

The palette, image, and motif support the event message; they are not decoration for its own sake.
