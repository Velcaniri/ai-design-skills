# Example: UX/UI Design

## Scenario

A ticket checkout asks for attendee name, email, ticket type, and quantity. The primary action is labeled “Continue.”

## Worked review

**Finding — Ticket selection is unclear (High)**

- **Location:** Ticket type and quantity section.
- **Evidence:** The selected ticket card is only marked with a small color change, and the total price is shown above the quantity control.
- **User impact:** A visitor may not know which ticket they selected or whether changing quantity updates the amount.
- **Recommendation:** Add a clear selected state with text or an icon, keep quantity beside the ticket choice, and show an updated total near the continue action.
- **Priority:** High because uncertainty affects payment readiness.

## Follow-up checks

Verify keyboard selection, visible focus, error feedback, narrow-screen layout, and whether the total updates after changing ticket type or quantity. A screenshot alone cannot confirm those behaviors.

This review uses choice, feedback, grouping, and target-size lenses; it does not claim the page violates a law based on appearance alone.
