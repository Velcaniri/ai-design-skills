# Example: Motion Design

## Brief

A 6-second intro for a student film festival needs to reveal the festival name and date without making the information hard to read.

## Motion concept

Use a **minimal smooth** reveal for the background shapes, followed by **kinetic typography** for the festival name. Let the type settle before bringing in the date. Keep the text static long enough to read, then end on a still title card.

## Timing to test

- 0–1 s: background shapes ease into place.
- 1–2.5 s: festival name appears and settles.
- 2.5–4.5 s: date fades in below the name.
- 4.5–6 s: hold the complete title card.

These are prototype timings. Check them with the actual font, audio, screen size, and viewing context.

## Accessibility check

Avoid flashing or strobing. Keep a static version available, and make sure the name and date remain available as text or captions when needed.
